About Acme Plumbing

Founded in 2004 by Dave Smith.

Dave started Acme with one van and a simple promise: turn up when we say we will, explain the problem in plain English and charge a fair price. Twenty years later that promise has not changed, even though the van has become a fleet of twelve.

We are still a family business. Dave's daughter Emma runs the office and bookings, and his son Tom leads our bathroom fitting team. Most of our customers come to us through recommendations from friends and neighbours, which we think says more than any advert.

Meet the team

Our engineers are Gas Safe registered.

Our team of fourteen engineers covers heating, drainage, leak detection and bathroom installation. Every engineer is fully insured, DBS checked and trained on the latest boilers and controls. We invest in training each year so the team keeps up with new regulations and energy-saving technology.

How we work

We give a clear price before any work begins, and we never add hidden extras. Our engineers wear shoe covers, protect your floors and clean up before they leave. Every repair is backed by a twelve-month guarantee on parts and labour.

Accreditations

Acme Plumbing is Gas Safe registered, a member of the Chartered Institute of Plumbing and Heating Engineering, and an approved installer for leading boiler brands. We are proud to hold a five-star rating from hundreds of local customers.

Our phone lines are staffed every day of the year, including bank holidays, so a real person always answers your call.
